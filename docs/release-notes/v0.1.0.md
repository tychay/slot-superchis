# slot. superchis v0.1.0: first test release

A cartridge-carousel home screen for the **SuperChis Prime** GBA flashcart. It is a fork of
[SuperFW](https://github.com/davidgfnet/superfw) v0.22 (commit `dabbf49`) with the
[slot.](https://github.com/slot-cfw/slot) carousel, ported from
[slot. advance](https://github.com/FrankieT19/slot-advance). Your games sit as carts on a shelf.
Pick one, it slides into the slot, and SuperFW launches it as usual: saves, patches, in-game menu
and the bundled GB/GBC emulator all work.

**This is a test release.** It has been tested on one SuperChis Prime in one original GBA, and
nowhere else. Reports from other hardware are very welcome.

## What's in it

- GBA, GB and GBC shelves read from `Games/GBA`, `Games/GB` and `Games/GBC`.
- Left/Right moves between carts (hold to scroll) and L/R switches shelf, with slot.'s spring motion.
- Label art from [slot. Cart Studio](https://studio.slot-cfw.fyi/), converted for slot.
- Wood, Dark and Light themes: hold **B** and press **Start**.
- Insert animation and sound.
- **Select** opens the stock SuperFW menu, so every SuperFW feature stays reachable.
  **Select** on its Settings, UI, Tools or Info tab returns to the carousel.
- Your shelf, cart and theme are remembered.

## Install (SD card only, nothing is flashed)

1. Your SuperChis must already run stock SuperFW from its flash.
2. Copy `slot-superchis-v0.1.0.gba` anywhere on the SD card.
3. Put your games, **unzipped**, in `Games/GBA`, `Games/GB` and `Games/GBC` at the card root.
4. Boot stock SuperFW and launch `slot-superchis-v0.1.0.gba` from its file browser.
5. Optional: label art from [slot. Cart Studio](https://studio.slot-cfw.fyi). Put an empty file
   named `ezkernel.bin` at the card root so Cart Studio treats the card as a slot. card, then
   use **Write to card** in Chrome or Edge. The README's
   [Label art](https://github.com/tychay/slot-superchis#label-art) section has the details.

To uninstall, delete the `.gba` file. Nothing else on the card or the cart changes.

## Known limitations

- **After you quit a game you land in stock SuperFW, not the carousel.** Exiting a game resets the
  cart into the flashed firmware. Stock SuperFW opens on its Recent tab, and the carousel's
  `.gba` stays at the top of it, so getting back is power on → **A**.
- GBA games show SuperFW's usual load screen after the insert animation. GB/GBC games start
  straight away.
- Label art can show up a moment late when scrolling fast, mostly on GB/GBC shelves.
- English only. SuperChis only: other SuperCard boards are not built.

## Flashing: untested

The author has not flashed this build and does not own a DS to recover a cart. Run it from the SD
card. If you have a DS or DS Lite, a Slot-1 flashcart and
[superfw-nds-flasher-tool](https://github.com/davidgfnet/superfw-nds-flasher-tool) to back up and
restore your flash, the README's
[Flashing](https://github.com/tychay/slot-superchis#flashing-untested) section has the steps, and
reports are very welcome.

## If something goes wrong

- **White screen after the "slot." splash:** run `slot-superchis-v0.1.0-diag.gba` (included). It
  draws rows of coloured squares that show which startup step or memory test failed. Take a photo
  and attach it to an issue.
- **Plain carts, no wood background:** the art pack failed to unpack, so please report it.
- Open an issue with your cart model, console (GBA, GBA SP, Micro, DS) and SD card size.
